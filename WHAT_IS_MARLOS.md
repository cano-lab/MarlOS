# What is MarlOS?

**MARLOS** = **M**emory **A**ugmented **R**esearch & **L**earning **O**perating **S**ystem

MarlOS is your **personal knowledge companion** - a desktop app that remembers your work, helps you think better, and keeps your information private.

---

## What the Name Means

| Letter | Stands For | What It Means for You |
|--------|------------|----------------------|
| **M** | **Memory** | Remembers everything you work on - documents, conversations, notes |
| **A** | **Augmented** | AI-augmented thinking - not replacing you, but amplifying your capabilities |
| **R** | **Research** | Collect sources, organize knowledge, generate papers with citations |
| **L** | **Learning** | Thinking Debugger helps you learn *how* to think, not just get answers |
| **O** | **Operating** | A platform for knowledge work - not just an app, but an environment |
| **S** | **System** | All your tools working together in one place |

---

## The Problem

Every day, you:
- Have conversations with AI assistants (ChatGPT, Claude, etc.)
- Read documents, PDFs, and ebooks
- Research topics across dozens of browser tabs
- Take notes that end up scattered everywhere
- Forget where you found that one important thing

**Your knowledge is fragmented.** It lives in different apps, different accounts, different devices. When you need something, you can't find it.

---

## What MarlOS Does

### 1. Remembers Everything You Work On

MarlOS creates a **semantic memory** of your work. Not just files - but the *meaning* behind them. Ask questions like:

- "What was I researching last Tuesday?"
- "Find that PDF about climate change"
- "What did the AI say about my project idea?"

MarlOS understands what you're looking for, not just keywords.

### 2. Keeps Your Data Private

Everything stays on **your computer**. No cloud accounts. No subscriptions. No company reading your documents.

- Your PDFs? On your machine.
- Your conversations? On your machine.
- Your notes? On your machine.

You own your data. Period.

### 3. Works With Your Local AI

MarlOS connects to AI running on your own computer (like LM Studio or Ollama). This means:

- No sending sensitive documents to OpenAI
- No monthly API costs
- Works offline
- Complete privacy

### 4. Helps You Think Better

The **Thinking Debugger** analyzes your conversations with AI and shows you where your thinking went wrong:

- **Wrong Facts** - Did you accept something false without questioning it?
- **Wrong Reasoning** - Was your logic flawed?
- **Wrong Questions** - Did you miss the real issue?

It's like having a critical thinking coach that helps you learn *how to think*, not just get answers.

---

## How MarlOS Brings Everything Together

Here's where MarlOS is different: **it connects your entire digital workflow.**

### One Place for All Your AI Conversations

You probably use multiple AI tools:
- **ChatGPT** for quick questions
- **Claude** for complex reasoning
- **Cursor** for coding help
- **Other AI assistants** for specific tasks

Each one is valuable, but they're isolated. Your ChatGPT history doesn't talk to your Cursor sessions. That brilliant insight from Claude? Locked away.

**MarlOS imports conversations from all these services** and stores them in your personal knowledge base. Suddenly:
- Your Cursor coding sessions inform your research papers
- Your ChatGPT brainstorming connects to your PDF notes
- Everything becomes searchable and connected

### Documents Meet Conversations

When you read a PDF in MarlOS, you can:
- Ask questions about it using your local AI
- Connect insights to conversations you've had
- Automatically extract citations for papers
- Build a semantic map of related concepts

Your documents aren't files anymore - they're part of your thinking.

### Research That Grows With You

The **Research Hub** helps you collect sources, organize notes, and generate papers automatically. But it also:

- Remembers what you've researched before
- Suggests connections between old and new projects
- Builds a personal knowledge graph over time
- Helps you avoid reinventing the wheel

### The Memory Layer

This is MarlOS's secret weapon: everything goes through a **semantic memory system**.

When you:
- Import a ChatGPT conversation → MarlOS remembers the concepts
- Read a PDF → MarlOS extracts the ideas
- Take notes → MarlOS links them to related work
- Have an AI chat → MarlOS captures the insights

Then when you search, you're not finding files - you're finding **meaning**.

Ask "What have I learned about machine learning?" and MarlOS returns:
- Relevant PDFs you've read
- Conversations where you discussed ML
- Notes you took on the topic
- Code you wrote in Cursor
- All ranked by what's actually useful to you

## The Philosophy

> "AI should amplify your growth, not replace your thinking."

MarlOS is built on the belief that AI is a powerful tool - like a hammer. But a hammer doesn't teach you carpentry. If you use AI to skip thinking, you never learn.

MarlOS helps you:
1. **Use AI effectively** - as a thinking partner, not a replacement
2. **Build knowledge** - that compounds over time
3. **Think critically** - about what AI tells you
4. **Stay in control** - of your data and your mind

## Getting Started

### First Time Setup

1. **Download MarlOS** for your computer (Windows, Mac, or Linux)
2. **Install a local AI** (like LM Studio or Ollama) - free and runs on your machine
3. **Open MarlOS** and connect it to your AI
4. **Import your existing data**:
   - Export your ChatGPT conversations
   - Add your Cursor coding sessions
   - Drop in PDFs and documents

### A Typical Day

```
Morning
├─ Import yesterday's ChatGPT conversation about a project idea
├─ MarlOS automatically remembers the key concepts
└─ Search shows related PDFs you read last month

Afternoon
├─ Open a research paper in MarlOS document viewer
├─ Chat with local AI to understand difficult sections
├─ Highlight important passages - automatically saved to memory
└─ MarlOS suggests connections to your previous work

Evening
├─ Use Thinking Debugger on a conversation that felt "off"
├─ Learn you asked the wrong question - get guidance on what to ask instead
├─ Start new conversation with better questions
└─ MarlOS remembers both attempts, creating a learning feedback loop
```

### The Feedback Loop

This is the real power of MarlOS:

1. **You work** - reading, chatting, researching
2. **MarlOS remembers** - capturing meaning, not just data
3. **You search** - finding connections you forgot you made
4. **You discover** - old ideas combine with new problems
5. **You grow** - your knowledge compounds over time

The more you use MarlOS, the smarter it gets about *your* thinking.

## Under the Hood (For the Curious)

MarlOS isn't magic - it's just smart architecture:

| Component | What It Does |
|-----------|--------------|
| **Importers** | Connect to ChatGPT, Cursor, Obsidian to bring in your data |
| **Semantic Memory** | Turns documents and conversations into searchable meaning |
| **Vector Database** | Finds related content by understanding concepts, not keywords |
| **Local AI Bridge** | Connects to LM Studio, Ollama, or any local LLM |
| **Thinking Debugger** | Analyzes conversations for reasoning errors |
| **Research Hub** | Manages sources and generates papers with citations |

All of this runs **locally on your machine**. No cloud. No subscriptions. No tracking.

**Your knowledge. Your computer. Your control.**
